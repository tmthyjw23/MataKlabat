# MataKlabat media sources

Download everything listed here with `python scripts/fetch_media.py`. Files are not committed to git; this list (with authors and licenses) is the source of truth. CC BY / CC BY-SA items require attribution when shown publicly (slides, report).

## Summary

- Generated 2026-10-27. Nothing was downloaded, no logins, Pexels cookie banner declined (Reject All).
- Test-set images: fire 12, smoke 12, fog 12, sunset 12, lights 12, normal 12 = 72 of 72.
- Demo photos: 8 listed (smoke slope 1, active fire on hillside 2, fog 1, sunset 1, Mount Klabat 1, burning trash 2).
- Videos (Pexels): 2 calm hillside + 1 forest fire = 3 of 3.
- Needs manual download: 0. All image URLs are direct upload.wikimedia.org files (1280 px thumbnail where the original is wider than 1280 px, otherwise the original file). All video URLs are direct videos.pexels.com 720p mp4 files (the Pexels `/download/video/<id>/?h=720&w=1280` link 302-redirects to the same file).
- All 80 images come from Wikimedia Commons. Licenses: Public domain, CC0, CC BY 2.0/2.5/3.0/4.0, CC BY-SA 2.0/3.0/4.0. CC BY / CC BY-SA items need attribution (author + license are in the tables). Pixabay was not needed.
- Problems and caveats:
  - Most fire and smoke images are US wildfires (public domain, US Forest Service / NPS) because Commons has few tropical ones. Tropical/Indonesian-region images: fire_10 (Sabah), fire_11 (Kalimantan), several fog (Malaysia, Sri Lanka) and normal (Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia) images.
  - Weak spots: `lights` has no strongly fire-like lights except lights_10; some sunset images are over dry/rocky hills rather than forest; smoke_08 is aerial; sunset_12 has a statue and antenna masts; fire_04 has a small date stamp; fire_08 is dark.
  - Burning trash: no free photo found with a person burning trash and a non-prominent face. The two listed show the burning pile and smoke only.
  - Images were judged from contact-sheet thumbnails (not downloaded). Metadata and license text come from the Commons API.
  - Same fire event, different photos: fire_01 and demo/api_lereng.jpg; smoke_01 and smoke_02.
  - Videos: the two calm-hillside clips are static-camera but show moving cloud/mist (no perfectly still forested hillside clip was found among the Pexels results checked). Alternates: Pexels 11796408 (misty conifer hillside, 32 s, static, 720p 60 fps, 7.8 MB, Ertabbt, https://www.pexels.com/video/smoke-in-forest-on-hill-11796408/ , file https://videos.pexels.com/video-files/11796408/11796408-hd_1280_720_60fps.mp4) and 8450827 (fog dissipating over forested mountain, 16 s, Arnav Kainthola, 5.5 MB).
  - Browser: Claude in Chrome was disconnected at first and then reconnected; a dedicated tab was used and closed at the end. HEAD checks of all image URLs were rate-limited (HTTP 429) by Wikimedia, so only a sample of 8 URLs was HEAD-verified (all HTTP 200, image/jpeg); the rest are the thumburl/url values returned by the Commons API.
